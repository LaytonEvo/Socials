The golf problem, and the setting that fixed most of it

At strength 1.0 the golf prompt failed three times in four and produced a visibly different woman. Her reference set is 87% portraits, so the model learned a portrait of her rather than a person — identity survives inside the framing the training data covers and drifts outside it.

Raising LoRA strength usually buys likeness by destroying prompt control, so the portraits were re-run as the check — they improved too, to 0.97948, within 0.005 of the ceiling. It is not a trade-off, so lora_scale: {{ lora_scale }} is now the configured default. Not pushed higher: three takes is three takes, and over-weighting eventually burns the likeness into every output whatever the prompt asks for.

Two things this does not say. Nothing here is video — it is stills, and whether the face survives animation is a separate question. And the golf image at 1.3 rendered an unprompted Nike swoosh on the visor, which is a trademark problem for the content policy guard rather than an identity one.
